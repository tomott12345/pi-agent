title: Exploring Brutalist
history: Brutalism is an architectural movement that emerged in the 1960s and 1970s, characterized by raw concrete, minimal ornamentation, and massive scale. It was inspired by the idea of "truth to materials" and the industrial landscape. Key works include the Barbican Centre in London, the Louvre Pyramid in Paris, and the Council for Tall Buildings and Urban Habitat (CTBUH) tower in Dubai.
features: - Massive concrete surfaces - Monolithic forms - Geometric shapes - Integration with landscape
