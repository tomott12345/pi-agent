# Art Impact Skill

**Description**  
Finds a piece of art that made an impact when it was created or discovered, and returns a concise two‑paragraph summary of the artist (or artwork) plus a direct link to the Wikipedia article.

**Usage**  
`art-impact "Artwork Title"`  
or  
`art-impact "Artist Name"`

**Example**  
`art-impact "Starry Night"`

**Output**  
Two paragraphs summarizing the art and its significance, followed by a link to the full Wikipedia page.

**Implementation**  
The skill ships with a `run.sh` script located in the same directory; it is executed as `art-impact "Query"`.
